How an engagement runs

From the first call to the submitted bid, with a fixed quote agreed before any writing starts.

  1. Step one

    Opportunity review and quote

    You send the tender reference. We read the specification and scoring criteria and give you an honest view on whether it is winnable, then a fixed fee.

  2. Step two

    Evidence gathering

    One structured session with your operational leads to capture staffing, systems, performance data and case studies. We do the writing, not you.

  3. Step three

    Drafting and internal review

    Responses drafted against the marking scheme, then scored by a second reader as an evaluator would. You see the draft with time left to comment.

  4. Step four

    Compliance check and submission

    Word counts, attachments, mandatory documents and portal fields checked line by line, submitted ahead of deadline, with a debrief afterwards.

About

Procurement is a discipline. We treat it like one.

Marhana & Co exists because capable businesses lose contracts for reasons that have nothing to do with their capability.

Public-sector buyers work to rules that reward precision: published criteria, fixed word counts, mandatory evidence, immovable deadlines. A business with ten years of flawless delivery can still score a two because the answer did not follow the question.

Our work sits between what you actually do and what the evaluation matrix asks you to prove. We interview your delivery leads, pull out the detail that carries marks, and write it in the order an evaluator reads.

We take on a deliberately small number of clients at a time. Bids are deadline-bound and capacity is finite; stretching further would show up in the score.

What clients can expect

Named ownership

One consultant owns your submission from kick-off to debrief. No handovers mid-deadline.

Honest go / no-go advice

If a tender is not winnable for you, we will say so before you spend the fee.

Capability you keep

Every engagement leaves behind reusable evidence your team can deploy on the next opportunity.

FAQ

Questions we are asked before the first call.

01

How much notice do you need before a deadline?

Two to three weeks is comfortable for a full submission. Reviews can be turned around in 48 hours. If the deadline is tighter, ask — we will tell you honestly whether it is deliverable.

02

How do you charge?

Fixed fee per submission, quoted once we have read the ITT. Retainers are monthly. No percentage of contract value, and no fee before you have seen the scope in writing.

03

We have never bid for public work. Is that a problem?

No. A good proportion of our clients are first-time bidders. We will tell you what needs to be in place before you start and handle the parts that are unfamiliar.

04

How much of our time will this take?

Typically one evidence session of ninety minutes with your delivery lead, plus a review of the draft. We do the writing; you supply the facts only you have.

05

Do you guarantee a win?

No one honestly can. Price, capacity and competition all sit outside our control. What we guarantee is a compliant submission that answers the question and evidences the claim.

06

Is our tender information confidential?

Entirely. We will sign your NDA, and we do not work for two bidders on the same opportunity.
