SECTION A — PURPOSE AND SCOPE
A.1  This policy states how Marhana & Co Consulting Ltd (the "Company") manages its own environmental impact. It covers all of the Company's operations, wherever performed, including work carried out from home workplaces and at client premises.
A.2  It is aligned to the principles of ISO 14001:2015 — context, leadership, planning, support, operation, evaluation and improvement — but the Company is not certified to that standard and does not represent itself as certified.
A.3  Where the Company drafts a Carbon Reduction Plan, an environmental management response or a social value commitment for a client, it does so under the separate templates and service boundaries recorded in the Terms of Engagement. In particular, the Company structures and drafts Carbon Reduction Plans; it does not calculate greenhouse gas emissions. Those figures are supplied by the client or by a carbon consultant instructed by the client.

SECTION D — COMMITMENTS
D.1 Measure
D.1.1  The Company records, for each financial year: electricity attributable to working hours at each home workplace; business travel by mode and distance; IT hardware purchased and disposed of; and cloud storage volume.
D.1.2  Where an exact figure is not available the Company uses a documented estimate, records the method, and states that it is an estimate. It does not present an estimate as a measurement.
D.1.3  Conversion to CO₂e, where required, uses the UK Government greenhouse gas conversion factors for the relevant year, and the version used is recorded.
D.2 Reduce
Meetings are held remotely unless attendance in person is necessary for the work or requested by the client.
Where travel is necessary, rail is used in preference to car, and domestic air travel is not used except where rail would make the engagement impossible.
No routine printing. Draft review, mark-up, engagement letters and signatures are electronic.
Equipment is set to power-saving defaults and is replaced on condition rather than on age.
Stored data is reviewed against the Retention and Disposal Schedule at least annually, which reduces storage as well as data protection risk.
D.3 Procure
D.3.1  Environmental performance is one of the criteria in supplier selection, alongside price, competence and information security.
D.3.2  Refurbished IT equipment is considered first where it is fit for purpose and carries adequate warranty and security support.
D.3.3  Cloud and software providers are assessed on their published renewable energy and net zero commitments at the point of selection and at renewal.
D.4 Waste
D.4.1  The waste hierarchy is applied in order: prevention, preparing for re-use, recycling, other recovery, disposal.
D.4.2  End-of-life IT equipment is transferred to an authorised treatment facility or an approved WEEE take-back scheme. It is not placed in general waste.
D.4.3  Before any device leaves the Company's control, data is securely erased or the storage medium is destroyed, and a certificate or record is obtained. That record satisfies both this policy and Section D of the Retention and Disposal Schedule, and the two are reconciled at each review.
D.4.4  Confidential paper waste is cross-cut shredded or destroyed under a certificated service. It is not recycled unshredded.
D.5 Influence — the part that matters most
D.5.1  The Company writes environmental and social value commitments that clients then become contractually bound to deliver. A commitment drafted to score well but which the client cannot deliver is an environmental harm as well as a contractual one, because it displaces a commitment that would have been delivered.
D.5.2  Accordingly the Company will not draft an environmental or carbon commitment that the client has not confirmed it can deliver, and records the client's confirmation. Commitments rejected on this basis are recorded at Section E of T16.
D.5.3  The Company will not draft a net zero, carbon neutral or offsetting claim for a client without seeing the underlying basis for it.
D.5.4  The Company does not calculate greenhouse gas emissions figures and will not insert a figure into a client's Carbon Reduction Plan that the client has not supplied and verified.

SECTION G — MONITORING, REVIEW AND IMPROVEMENT
G.1  Performance against Section E is recorded at Annex A and reviewed at least annually.
G.2  Environmental performance is a standing item at the management review recorded under the Quality Management Policy.
G.3  This policy is reviewed at least annually and additionally on any change in the law, any change in the Company's operating model, and any failure to meet a target.
G.4  A failure to meet a target is recorded, with the reason, rather than removed. A target quietly deleted is worse evidence than a target missed and explained.
SECTION H — WHAT THE COMPANY DOES NOT CLAIM
H.1  This section exists because environmental claims to customers are regulated, and because a supplier that overstates its position in a tender exposes itself to exclusion and to a claim.
H.2  The Company is not certified to ISO 14001, ISO 50001 or any equivalent environmental management standard.
H.3  The Company does not claim to be carbon neutral or net zero, and does not purchase or claim carbon offsets.
H.4  The Company does not hold an independently verified greenhouse gas inventory. Figures reported at Annex A are self-reported and unassured, and are described as such wherever they are used.
H.5  Any environmental claim the Company makes in marketing or in a tender response must be accurate, substantiated, clear about what it covers, and capable of being evidenced, in accordance with the CMA Green Claims Code. A claim that cannot meet those tests is not made.
