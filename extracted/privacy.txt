SECTION A — WHO WE ARE
Marhana & Co Consulting Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales under company number 17460766, with its registered office at [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS]. We provide public-sector bid and tender support services to small and medium-sized enterprises.
We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office under registration number [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER].

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as we are not required to do so under Article 37 of the UK GDPR. Responsibility for data protection rests with [DATA PROTECTION LEAD NAME], [DATA PROTECTION LEAD ROLE], contactable at [DATA PROTECTION EMAIL].

SECTION F — SPECIAL CATEGORY AND CRIMINAL OFFENCE DATA
We do not routinely process special category data. Submission material occasionally contains it — for example workforce diversity statistics, accessibility adjustments, or records relating to health and safety. Where that occurs we process it only on the client's instruction, only to the extent required by the tender, and we rely on the client's own lawful basis and Article 9 condition.
Where a submission requires information about criminal convictions, including exclusion-ground declarations under the Procurement Act 2023, we record only what the client states and only for the purposes of that submission.
SECTION G — WHEN WE ACT AS A PROCESSOR
Where we handle personal data that belongs to a client and we do so only on their documented instructions — typically personnel details supplied for inclusion in a submission — we act as a processor and the client is the controller. In those circumstances:
We process the data only as instructed
We enter into terms meeting Article 28 of the UK GDPR before processing begins
We assist the client with data subject requests and with breach notification
We return or delete the data at the end of the engagement, at the client's election
If you are an individual whose data has been given to us by a client, the client's own privacy notice governs that processing. We will pass any request you make to them.
